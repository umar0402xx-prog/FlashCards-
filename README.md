# A Level Recall

Flashcards for Cambridge A Level Physics (9702), Chemistry (9701) and Biology (9700), with spaced
repetition, typeset maths and chemistry equations, and offline use.

## Using it on your phone

1. Open the app's link in Chrome or Samsung Internet.
2. Open the browser menu and tap **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).
3. Open it from your home screen. After the first load it works without internet.

## Studying

- **Start review** shows the cards that are due plus a few new ones each day.
- Tap the card to see the answer, then rate how well you remembered it:
  **Again** (you'll see it again in a few cards), **Hard**, **Good** or **Easy**. The label under each
  button shows when the card comes back.
- **Practise all** in a deck shuffles every card without changing your schedule. Good before a test.

## Getting new cards

Ask Claude to add cards for a topic. Claude edits the deck files in this repo, and the app picks them
up next time you open it online.

You can also add cards yourself in the app. They're saved on your phone only, so use
**Settings → Copy my cards for Claude** now and then and paste them into a chat with Claude to save them
in the repo.

## Your progress

Progress is stored on your phone. Use **Settings → Copy backup** every so often and keep the text
somewhere safe.
